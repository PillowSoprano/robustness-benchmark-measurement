# Additional analysis

The primary four-reference study comprises 120 cells, 116 evaluable. The TEP observation-bias extension adds two levels (3 and 4), reported separately. `rescore.py --system tep_bias_cap` reconstructs its curves, crossing brackets and bootstrap intervals from the frozen per-episode losses; the default command also includes it.

`python loto.py` recomputes the eight leave-one-MBR-trajectory-out curves and crossing summaries. These are sensitivity estimates, not confidence intervals.

The TS-Fault reanalysis in `../reanalysis/tsfault_common_reference.py` recomputes own r from faulted mean / clean mean. Source Sec. V-D describes its separately printed r as a mean of within-dataset ratios. This yields rho -0.414, p 0.062, while the common-reference result remains +0.052, p 0.823. Source data, identity check and all21 rows are in `summary/tsfault_audit.json`.

The code/figures in this local revision snapshot are the exact accompanying version. Public repository synchronization is a separate action. Frozen-loss rescoring needs NumPy; the TS-Fault correlation uses SciPy. Retraining and simulator replay require the raw process inputs, checkpoints and simulator dependencies documented by the repository; they are not included in the compact archive.
